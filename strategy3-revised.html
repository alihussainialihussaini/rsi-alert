Profit after entry: how far did price go my way?

For every closed signal the page records the best price reached in your favour after entry (peak profit) and the worst price against you (max drawdown), even if the target was never hit. Long: highest price minus entry. Short: entry minus lowest price. Tracking stops when the trade closes (target, stop-loss or timeout). In the candle that hits the stop-loss, the favourable move is not counted, so the peaks shown are conservative. Only signals logged by this version are included.

Revised version with a profit-after-entry report. Signals use only CLOSED candles (nothing repaints). TP/SL are checked against every 1-minute candle high/low since entry; if one candle touches both, it counts as a loss. 45m is built from native 15m candles, 8m from 1m candles. Results are shown in R (target = +RR, stop = −1R) after fees on both sides. Signals with a stop that is too tight or too wide are skipped, and the same setup cannot re-fire during the cooldown. Methodology: higher timeframe RSI sets direction (bullish = RSI>50 and rising), lower timeframe RSI sets entry timing (pullback, turn up, momentum candle, resistance reclaim). On conflict the higher timeframe wins — a lower-timeframe trigger against the higher-timeframe bias is tagged "Conflict" and, unless you tick the Conflict option in the toolbar, logged nowhere. Confluence badges: Vol = current volume above its rolling average, Struct = higher-timeframe swing structure (HH/HL or LH/LL), Sweep = a wick through the recent swing extreme that closed back inside it. Elite requires all three plus a clearly-rising HTF RSI; Strong requires the HTF/LTF alignment alone; Medium requires HTF above 50 but not clearly rising; Weak/Conflict are not logged. Timeframes not native to Bybit (8m, 45m) are built by resampling 1-minute candles — these need a short warm-up window after Start before RSI stabilizes. Data: Bybit public v5 market API, no key required. This is a pattern-matching research tool against public price data only — it has no way to detect, and makes no claim about, a move that is being organized rather than discovered.
