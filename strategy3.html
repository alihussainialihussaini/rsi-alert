Methodology: higher timeframe RSI sets direction (bullish = RSI>50 and rising), lower timeframe RSI sets entry timing (pullback, turn up, momentum candle, resistance reclaim). On conflict the higher timeframe wins — a lower-timeframe trigger against the higher-timeframe bias is tagged "Conflict" and logged nowhere. Confluence badges: Vol = current volume above its rolling average, Struct = higher-timeframe swing structure (HH/HL or LH/LL), Sweep = a wick through the recent swing extreme that closed back inside it. Elite requires all three plus a clearly-rising HTF RSI; Strong requires the HTF/LTF alignment alone; Medium requires HTF above 50 but not clearly rising; Weak/Conflict are not logged. Timeframes not native to Bybit (8m, 45m) are built by resampling 1-minute candles — these need a short warm-up window after Start before RSI stabilizes. Data: Bybit public v5 market API, no key required. This is a pattern-matching research tool against public price data only — it has no way to detect, and makes no claim about, a move that is being organized rather than discovered. Peak favorable % tracks the best price reached in your favor since entry, even if the trade never hits target and later reverses — shown per signal in the journal and summarized for still-open signals in the "Open positions" report above.
