How to read it. Each cell shows sell × buy volume at that price: sell is volume hitting the bid, buy is volume lifting the ask. Δ is buy minus sell for the candle, Σ its total. Green bold = buy imbalance (ask volume at least the set ratio of the bid one row below). Red bold = sell imbalance. Amber outline = most-traded price (POC).
Absorption = volume well above average, concentrated at one extreme with sellers (or buyers) dominating there, yet price closes away from it and delta does not follow. It becomes a signal after a later candle closes beyond the absorption candle with matching delta. A close beyond the other side cancels it. Footprint data starts from the last ~1000 trades when the page opens; candles marked * are partial and are never scored.
