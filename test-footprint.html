How to read it. Each cell shows sell × buy volume at that price: sell is volume hitting the bid, buy is volume lifting the ask. Δ is buy minus sell for the candle, Σ its total. Green bold = buy imbalance (ask volume at least the set ratio of the bid one row below). Red bold = sell imbalance. Amber outline = most-traded price (POC).
CVD (cumulative volume delta) is the running total of Δ, drawn as candles from the first candle with tick data. CVD divergence: bullish when price makes a lower low while CVD makes a higher low, bearish when price makes a higher high while CVD makes a lower high. It is drawn on the price chart and on the CVD line between the two pivots, and is confirmed once the set number of candles after the second pivot have closed. A divergence next to an absorption signal adds a CVD div ✓ tag to it. The #n under each time is the candle count since footprint data began.
Absorption = volume well above average, concentrated at one extreme with sellers (or buyers) dominating there, yet price closes away from it and delta does not follow. It becomes a signal after a later candle closes beyond the absorption candle with matching delta. A close beyond the other side cancels it. Footprint data starts from the last ~1000 trades when the page opens; candles marked * are partial and are never scored. Candles without footprint data show as plain candles.
